Homoset vs. mixle: reconciling the GuthrieSolv dump

Two ways to turn 53,895 noisy literature measurements into one trustworthy ΔGhyd per molecule — a deterministic PS-gate consensus vs. a hierarchical partial-pooling model — scored against FreeSolv.

GuthrieSolv is the largest extracted hydration dataset but ships no reconciliation code (only a SMILES-parse test). Raw values live in 172 unit strings across vapour-pressure / Henry / solubility / free-energy measurements; all were harmonized to Ben-Naim ΔGhyd (FreeSolv's standard state), temperature-corrected, keyed by InChIKey.

Cleaning the units: a rule engine, not a regex

The 172 unit strings are converted by a declarative dimensional-analysis rule engine (unit_rules.py) that replaced the brittle regex switchboard: de-wrap log/ln/−log → tokenise into ⟨prefix⟩⟨base⟩power factors → reduce to a net dimension signature (pressure · amount · volume · mass · mole-fraction · energy) → classify into a physical quantity → convert. Adding a unit is one table row, never a new branch.

Four parsing bugs the switchboard hid were the reason to rebuild: mole-fraction Henry off by RT·ln1000; a process-dependent Henry-ratio direction (KWG invert / KGW keep); a one-char regex (^log10? needs "log1") that silently dropped every log(M)/log(atm) spelling; and mole-fraction solubility. The engine also resolves the mass-fraction-vs-Henry-ratio ambiguity by process (AQSOL = solubility fraction; KWG/KGW = Henry ratio). The ~10 exotic named strings it can't infer (MPv/(RTCw), logK=y/x at 1 atm) are exactly what the active-learning loop below calibrates — deterministic rules + data-driven fallback.

Iterative fixed-point loop for the long-tail units

The hand-coded switchboard handles the frequent units; an active-learning loop recovers much of the rest without hand-coding each one — reusing the Homoset noise gate as the acceptance test. It admits a unit only when its calibration against already-converted anchors both correlates (|R| ≥ Rmin) and passes RMS/L ≤ η. Run as a fixed-point co-training loop: reconcile → the consensus becomes cleaner anchors → anneal the gate one notch looser (strict backbone first) → repeat until a pass at the loosest gate is dry.

Why not all 172 units? Physics, not a limitation

A fair question — unit conversion is "just math", so why stop at 26 units? Because only some of the 172 strings are actually units of ΔGhyd.

Convertible = Henry + free energy

The Henry-constant and free-energy families are molecule-independent conversions of ΔGhyd — and every one is now converted. A declarative dimensional-analysis rule engine (unit_rules.py) replaced the brittle regex switchboard: de-wrap → tokenise → net dimensions → classify → convert. It handles 65 unit combos (vs 57) at MAE 0.275 / R 0.972; adding a unit is one table row, not a new branch.

Not convertible = VP & solubility

The remaining ~35,000 rows are vapour pressure and solubility — different observables. No math maps a lone vapour pressure to ΔGhyd (it needs volatility and solubility). These convert only by pairing (1,722 molecules); 750 VP-only + 1,666 solubility-only molecules stay lost — a data gap, not a math gap.

The hierarchical model halves the raw-median error on conflicts because it corrects each source's estimated bias and shrinks contaminated molecules toward the global prior, instead of letting the median sit among bad values.

Homoset's two parameters: noise level η and dimension L

The whole method is two knobs. A source joins the homogeneous set iff its relative scatter clears the noise level: noise = RMS/L ≤ η, i.e. PS = 1 − RMS/L ≥ 1 − η. The per-source noise makes the behaviour explicit:

On clean data PAIR is the least-trusted converted source — highest gate noise (0.16) and the largest mixle bias (+0.91) — so the two methods still independently agree on the worst source, just a different one.

Verdict

Different tools for different jobs — and they compose.

Homoset — the curator

Transparent triage

Two knobs — noise level η and dimension L. Anchor L to real experimental noise (0.6) and the gate becomes a source-quality filter; its per-molecule homogeneity flag gives a direct use_for_training decision. Accuracy hinges on (η, L); with the data-adaptive L it barely beats a median.

mixle — the reconciler

Lowest error, no knobs

Best RMSE and bias everywhere. Keeps all data, learns and corrects per-source bias, down-weights outliers, and uniquely rescues molecules with no clean reference. Cost: less interpretable — no explicit reject-this-source or curate-this-molecule flag.

Recommendation

Use both

Reconcile values with mixle's bias-correction + robust weights; triage with Homoset's PS gate and range flag to decide which molecules a human should re-check. GuthrieSolv's redundancy is real signal — both methods extract it, the raw mean does not.
